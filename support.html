Support

Help for installation, use, and troubleshooting.

Makarios Development keeps Westminster Catechizer simple to install and straightforward to support. This page provides a direct contact point and a practical overview of common support needs.

Contact support

For help with Westminster Catechizer, email makariosdevelopment@gmail.com.

When reporting a problem, it is helpful to include your device model, Android version, and a short description of what happened.

Common support topics

  • Questions about installing the APK or accessing the Play Store listing
  • Help understanding profile, statistics, or memorization features
  • Bug reports related to answer checking or navigation
  • Feedback on study flow, readability, or usability improvements

Installation guidance

If you are installing from the Play Store, use the official store listing once it is published. If you are installing by APK, follow the instructions provided with the download link and confirm that your device permits the installation method you have chosen.

Study and content questions

Westminster Catechizer is a memorization tool for the Westminster Shorter Catechism. For pastoral or doctrinal questions beyond app usage, users should consult their pastor, elders, teachers, or trusted theological resources.

Response expectations

Support responses are typically handled by email. If you plan to publish the app broadly, this page can later be updated with expected response times or additional support channels.
