What is the Westminster Shorter Catechism?

A historic summary of Christian doctrine in question-and-answer form.

The Westminster Shorter Catechism was written in the seventeenth century as a concise teaching instrument for instructing believers in the faith. It addresses God, Scripture, man's chief end, sin, redemption, the moral law, the sacraments, prayer, and the ordinary means of grace.

For many Reformed Christians, it remains a trusted guide for family worship, personal discipleship, and the doctrinal instruction of children and new believers.

Why an app for catechism study?

Memorization is strengthened by repetition, testing, and regular review. Westminster Catechizer makes those habits easier to maintain by combining the catechism text with answer checking, progress visibility, and simple navigation on a device many people already use every day.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for parents, students, and church members.

Who is Westminster Catechizer for?

The app is suited for individual Christians, families, pastors, teachers, and congregations who want a straightforward way to review and memorize the Westminster Shorter Catechism.

Does the app include the full Westminster Shorter Catechism?

Yes. Westminster Catechizer includes all 107 questions and answers so users can work through the entire catechism in one place.

How does the memorization system work?

Users read the question, type the answer from memory, review accuracy, retry difficult material, and reveal the answer when needed before moving on to the next question.

Can more than one person use the app on the same device?

Yes. Multiple user profiles allow different students to keep their own progress, memorized counts, and statistics separate.

Is the app appropriate for children and families?

The app is built with a clean and family-friendly presentation intended to support Christian instruction in homes, schools, and churches.
