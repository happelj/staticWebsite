Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains how Westminster Catechizer handles user data in connection with the Android app and this static website.

Overview

Westminster Catechizer is intended to help users memorize and learn the Westminster Shorter Catechism. The app is designed to function without requiring account creation or a web-based user dashboard.

Information stored by the app

The app may store study-related information on your device so that your progress can be preserved between sessions. This may include:

  • User profile names created within the app
  • Accuracy percentages and study statistics
  • Memorized question tracking
  • Total study time and other local progress information

Personal information

Westminster Catechizer is not designed to require personal accounts, subscription profiles, or user-submitted personal identity information in order to use the core study features.

Data sharing

Study data stored by the app is intended for app functionality and is not presented as information sold to third parties. If you install the app through a third-party marketplace such as Google Play, that platform may collect technical or transactional data under its own policies.

Children and family use

Westminster Catechizer is designed to be family-friendly and may be used by parents, children, and church members for educational purposes. Parents and guardians should supervise device usage as they deem appropriate.

Security

Reasonable care should be taken to protect the device on which the app is installed. Data stored locally on the device is subject to the security of that device and its operating environment.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated if Westminster Catechizer adds new functionality, distribution methods, or data practices. Changes should be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be sent to makariosdevelopment@gmail.com.
