Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Westminster Catechizer handles information in connection with the Android application and related static website.

Local-only storage Study progress is stored on-device for app functionality.
No required accounts No account creation or cloud dashboard is needed.
No sale of personal data Study information is not sold to third parties.

Overview

Westminster Catechizer is designed to help users memorize and study the Westminster Shorter Catechism. The app is intended to function without requiring account creation, cloud synchronization, or a web-based user dashboard.

Information Stored on Your Device

The app may store study-related information locally on your device in order to preserve progress between sessions. This information may include:

  • User profile names created within the app
  • Accuracy percentages and study statistics
  • Memorized question tracking
  • Total study time and related progress information
  • App preferences such as dark mode settings

This information is stored locally on the device and is intended solely for app functionality.

Personal Information

Westminster Catechizer does not require users to create personal accounts or submit personally identifiable information in order to use the core features of the app.

The app is not designed to collect sensitive personal information such as:

  • Government identification numbers
  • Financial information
  • Precise location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos or media files

Data Sharing and Third Parties

Westminster Catechizer does not sell personal information or study data to third parties.

If the app is distributed through third-party platforms such as Google Play, those services may collect technical, diagnostic, or transactional information according to their own privacy policies.

Children and Family Use

Westminster Catechizer is designed to be family-friendly and may be used by parents, children, churches, schools, and individuals for educational and religious study purposes. Parents and guardians are responsible for supervising device usage as appropriate.

Security

Data stored by the app is subject to the security of the device and operating environment on which the app is installed. Users are encouraged to use reasonable security protections on their devices.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if Westminster Catechizer adds new functionality, distribution methods, or data practices. Updates will be reflected on this page.
